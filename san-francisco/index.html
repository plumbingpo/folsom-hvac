Complete Heating & Cooling Services for San Francisco Properties

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: aging furnaces on the older streets, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and rooftop units on commercial buildings. Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. That small step can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Francisco

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
